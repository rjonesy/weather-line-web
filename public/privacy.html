Weather Line privacy

Updated November 16, 2026.

Your saved data

Saved cities, their forecast coordinates, preferences, and saved forecasts stay in this browser or installed Home Screen app. They are not synchronized with an account. Remove cities in Settings, or clear this website’s data to erase its local saved data.

Current location and street address

When you allow location access, the browser supplies your position. The app sends forecast coordinates to its hosted service and Apple WeatherKit for weather. For a street address, it sends the position through its hosted service to Apple Maps. Street-address results are displayed temporarily in memory and are not saved as part of your saved locations. Declining location access still allows city search.

Other services

City searches are sent to Open-Meteo’s city-search service. The hosting service and these providers process requests and connection information under their own policies. The app does not add advertising or analytics trackers.
